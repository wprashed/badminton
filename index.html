Quick Start: Get Started in 30 Seconds

Badminton Cup is built for casual players, school students, and professional tournament directors. You can jump directly into matches, open challenges for classmates or colleagues, bet virtual coins on match winners, or manage full multi-court tournament brackets.

1

Open Challenges

Head to the Challenges tab to post an open match invite. Enter your name, choose Singles or Doubles, set target points (e.g. 21 pts), and write your court location.

✓ Open to all general users & students
2

Instant Winner & Scoring

On any match card on the Matches tab, you can record a game in 3 ways:

  • [🏆 Team Won]: 1-tap instant 2-0 victory.
  • [⚡ Score]: Type exact final set scores (e.g. 21–18).
  • [🏸 Referee]: Point-by-point live umpire with speech.

✓ Instant automatic coin payouts on match win!

PWA & Push Notifications

Install the app on your phone via the + Install button or browser menu. Tap the 🔔 bell in the top bar to enable live alerts for match finishes and open challenges.

21-Point Rally Scoring System

A match consists of the best of 3 games of 21 points. The side winning a rally adds a point to its score regardless of who served.

Deuce Rule: At 20-all, the side which gains a 2-point lead first wins the game (e.g. 22-20, 24-22).
Sudden Death Cap: At 29-all, the side scoring the 30th point wins the game (30-29 max cap).

Service Courts & Diagonal Serving

Service court position is determined strictly by the server's score:

0 or Even Score (0, 2, 4, 6...): Serve from the RIGHT service court into the diagonal right court.
Odd Score (1, 3, 5, 7...): Serve from the LEFT service court into the diagonal left court.

Intervals & Change of Ends

Standard rest and coaching intervals:

11-Point Interval: A 60-second break occurs when the leading score reaches 11 points in any game.
Between Games: A 120-second (2-minute) interval is allowed between games.
Deciding 3rd Game: Players change ends when the leading score reaches 11 points.

Card Penalties & Disciplinary Code

The umpire administers official cards for misconduct or intentional delay:

🟨 Yellow Card: Warning for misconduct or time wasting.
🟥 Red Card: Fault awarded against offender; 1 point awarded to opponent.
⬛ Black Card: Disqualification from tournament (awarded by Referee).

Q: How do I start a tournament from scratch?

Switch to Admin mode (or sign in as Director), open the Menu, and select Setup & Roster. You can click "Load Pro Demo Roster" to instantly populate 8 world champions, or enter your own custom player roster and click "Start Tournament".

Q: Can I use this app without an internet connection?

Yes! Badminton Cup is a full Progressive Web App (PWA) with a Service Worker cache. Once loaded, you can score matches, flip coins, view rules, and record results completely offline in a gym or school court.

Q: Are coins real money?

No. Coins are 100% virtual fun points for fan predictions and school friendly competitions. There are no real money payments or microtransactions. You receive 100 free coins and can claim 50 bonus coins anytime.

Q: How does the Playoff Bracket work?

After group matches finish, top players from Pool A and Pool B are seeded into the knockout bracket (Quarter-Finals, Semi-Finals, and Grand Final). Tap the winner on each bracket match card to automatically advance them to the next round until the Champion is crowned!
